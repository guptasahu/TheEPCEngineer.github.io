Piping Data Tool

Piping Component Selection & Dimensional Drawing

Preliminary selection for pipe, flanges, fittings, gaskets, valves and line blanks. Enter project design data and licensed standard dimensions to generate a component schedule and scaled schematic.

Engineering limitation: ASME B16.5 does not size every piping component. This tool maps each component to the correct standard and performs preliminary checks. Final dimensions and pressure-temperature ratings must be verified from the licensed project-controlled standard and piping material specification.

1. Design conditions

2. Component selection

3. Pressure-class rating inputs

Enter allowable pressure at the design temperature from the licensed ASME B16.5 material-group table. The tool selects the lowest class whose allowable pressure is at least the design pressure.

4. Pipe-wall calculation inputs

Preliminary straight-pipe pressure design equation. Use allowable stress, weld quality factor and coefficient from the project-controlled ASME B31 code.

5. Licensed dimensional data

Enter dimensions from the applicable controlled standard, vendor drawing or approved piping data library. The diagram scales from these values.

Component result

Complete the design inputs and generate the selection.

Dimensional drawing

Schematic only. Dimensions are user-entered and must be verified against controlled standards.

About Me

Engineering should be rigorous, understandable and useful.

My work sits at the intersection of rotating-equipment engineering, EPC project execution, vendor-document review and professional development. I translate technical requirements into clear review logic, explain the engineering reasoning behind decisions and help engineers communicate recommendations with confidence.

My technical knowledge areas include centrifugal and positive-displacement pumps, compressors, turbines, blowers, mechanical seals, vibration analysis, reliability engineering, asset management and applicable API standards.

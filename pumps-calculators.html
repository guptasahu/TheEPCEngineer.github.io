Complete Centrifugal Pump Design Sequence

  1. Establish fluid properties and minimum, normal, rated and maximum duties.
  2. Select preliminary suction and discharge line sizes; check velocity and Reynolds number.
  3. Calculate Darcy friction factors, straight-pipe losses and fitting losses.
  4. Add static elevation, pressure-head, control-valve and equipment losses to determine TDH.
  5. Generate the system curve and identify the operating point using the vendor pump curve.
  6. Calculate NPSH available at the governing minimum-level, maximum-temperature and maximum-flow case.
  7. Calculate hydraulic power, shaft power, maximum absorbed power and preliminary motor rating.
  8. Check normal and rated flows against BEP and the vendor preferred operating region.
  9. Confirm minimum continuous stable flow, minimum thermal flow and recycle requirement.
  10. Check shutoff pressure, maximum suction pressure, maximum discharge pressure and pressure ratings.
  11. Apply affinity laws only within their valid range and verify against manufacturer curves.
  12. Finalize the datasheet, system curve, NPSH margin, motor, minimum-flow protection and vendor evaluation.
Important: Results are preliminary and do not replace API, ASME, IEC, ISO, project specifications, vendor guarantees, process simulation or a formal design review. Verify units and use absolute pressure for gas calculations.
Equation and unit references: SI conversion practice follows NIST guidance; pump-assessment context follows the U.S. Department of Energy MEASUR resources; ideal-gas compressor relations follow standard isentropic relations described by NASA Glenn Research Center.

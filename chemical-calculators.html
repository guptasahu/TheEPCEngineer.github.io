Chemical Injection Package Calculation Steps

Use the sequence below from process basis through equipment and operating checks. Each step identifies the required inputs, calculation, output and the calculator to use.

  1. 01

    Confirm process and chemical design basis

    Collect minimum, normal and maximum process flow, process density, design dosage, injection-point pressure, temperature, elevation, chemical concentration, solution density, viscosity, vapor pressure, required autonomy and operating philosophy.

    Output: approved calculation input sheet and operating cases.
  2. 02

    Calculate active chemical mass demand

    mactive = Dose × Qprocess × ρprocess / 10⁶. Calculate minimum, normal and maximum demand using the corresponding process-flow and dosage cases.

    Open Active Chemical Mass Rate
  3. 03

    Calculate commercial solution injection rate

    Qsolution = mactive /(ρsolution × active fraction). Use actual product concentration and density, not pure chemical properties.

    Open Dosage to Injection Solution Flow
  4. 04

    Establish minimum, normal and maximum pump duties

    Repeat the solution-flow calculation for all operating cases. Include intermittent or shock-dose cases separately. Do not average a short-duration peak into continuous normal flow.

    Output: Qmin, Qnormal and Qmax.
  5. 05

    Select preliminary rated pump capacity

    Qrated = Qmax × (1 + project margin). Apply the project-approved margin and then select a vendor capacity that keeps normal operation in a stable, controllable range.

    Open Metering Pump Design Capacity
  6. 06

    Check turndown, stroke and speed

    Verify that minimum duty is above the vendor minimum reliable setting and that normal duty is not continuously at an undesirable extreme. Consider multiple pump sizes or variable speed where one pump cannot cover the range.

    Open Turndown Check Open Stroke Setting
  7. 07

    Calculate required discharge pressure

    Add maximum injection-point pressure, static head, injection-line friction, injection quill/check-valve losses and project pressure margin. Use the maximum credible simultaneous condition.

    Open Required Pump Discharge Pressure
  8. 08

    Check suction conditions and NPSH/NPIPA

    Evaluate minimum tank level, vapor pressure at maximum temperature, suction piping losses, acceleration head and pulsation effects. Confirm vendor NPSHR/NPIPA criteria with the selected controlled-volume pump and suction dampener arrangement.

    Output: adequate suction margin for all operating cases.
  9. 09

    Size the chemical storage tank

    Vgross = Qinjection × autonomy / usable fraction. Base the duty on the governing consumption case, then account for low-level reserve, unusable volume, high-level allowance and batch-makeup needs.

    Open Chemical Tank Gross Volume Open Tank Autonomy Check
  10. 10

    Calculate batch dilution or makeup quantities

    Use a consistent mass or volume concentration basis. For simple compatible volume dilution, apply C1V1 = C2V2. For weight-based preparation, use density and mass balance.

    Open Batch Dilution
  11. 11

    Estimate hydraulic power and motor coordination

    Calculate hydraulic and shaft power, then obtain the vendor motor rating considering gearbox losses, starting torque, service factor, electrical standard and minimum project motor size.

    Open Pump Power
  12. 12

    Coordinate pulsation and relief protection

    Obtain vendor pulsation analysis or dampener sizing for the selected pump type, speed, number of heads, liquid properties and piping. Confirm relief-valve capacity, set pressure and safe return destination. These are vendor/package design checks and are not finalized by a simple generic formula.

    Output: dampener, PSV and minimum-flow/return philosophy.
  13. 13

    Check inventory, refill and package loads

    Calculate operating mass, refill interval, delivery size and tank/package operating loads for civil, structural and logistics coordination.

    Open Inventory Mass Open Refill Frequency
  14. 14

    Verify actual pump flow during testing

    Use calibration-column drawdown and test duration to establish actual output. Compare actual flow with required flow and calculate achieved dosage.

    Open Calibration Flow Open Achieved Dosage
  15. 15

    Finalize package datasheets and interfaces

    Document pump flow and pressure cases, tank volume, materials, seal/diaphragm arrangement, relief and pulsation devices, instruments, hazardous-area requirements, utilities, drains, vents, injection quill, piping loads, inspection and testing. Confirm all selections against project specifications and vendor data.

    Deliverables: datasheet, P&ID, package specification, MR, TBE inputs, calculation note and interface schedule.
Important: Results are preliminary and do not replace API, ASME, IEC, ISO, project specifications, vendor guarantees, process simulation or a formal design review. Verify units and use absolute pressure for gas calculations.
Equation and unit references: SI conversion practice follows NIST guidance; pump-assessment context follows the U.S. Department of Energy MEASUR resources; ideal-gas compressor relations follow standard isentropic relations described by NASA Glenn Research Center.
